## 一句話重點

以 NMDA 受體拮抗劑 ketamine 擾動腦區，發現預設模式網路與任務正向網路之間的反相關關係被破壞，並把這個結果連到認知與思覺失調。

## English summary

This PNAS study administered the NMDA receptor antagonist ketamine and examined how the default-mode and task-positive networks interact during tasks. It reports that ketamine disrupted the reciprocal, anticorrelated relationship between these two systems in task-dependent activation. The authors connect this to cognition and schizophrenia, but the task design and sample details are not confirmed here.

## 與本專案的關係

這篇與 30 同屬 E/I 與 NMDA 相關的機制研究，可作為「興奮性受體擾動 → 網路層級效應」的概念參考。但它處理的是人腦大尺度網路，與本專案 256 顆神經元的局部網路尺度不同，只適合作為概念對照。
