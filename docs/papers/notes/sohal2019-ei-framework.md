## 一句話重點

把 E/I 平衡定位為研究神經精神疾病機制的組織框架，並依近年的迴路知識更新 2003 年的自閉症假說。

## English summary

This review refines the concept of excitation-inhibition balance and updates the 2003 autism hypothesis with recent circuit knowledge. It discusses developmental mechanisms that reduce inhibition and how some autism-linked gene mutations disrupt inhibition in mice. The authors propose E/I balance as an organizing framework for pathophysiology and treatment, but the full text was not accessed, so the details are not confirmed here.
