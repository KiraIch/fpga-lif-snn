## 一句話重點

提出 STDP 受多巴胺調控，可以跨越延遲獎勵的時間差，把獎勵與先前的神經活動連起來（distal reward problem）。

## English summary

This paper proposes that spike-timing-dependent plasticity modulated by dopamine can resolve the distal reward problem. In a cortical spiking network, the slow kinetics of plasticity are sensitive to dopamine changes over a window of a few seconds. The full text was not accessed, so the model details and numbers are not confirmed here.
