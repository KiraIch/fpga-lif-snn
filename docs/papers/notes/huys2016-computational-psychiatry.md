## 一句話重點

計算精神醫學分為資料驅動與理論驅動兩條路線，兩者結合才能把神經科學的機制連到臨床應用。

## English summary

Huys, Maia and Frank review computational psychiatry as two complementary routes: data-driven machine learning and theory-driven mechanistic models. They argue that combining both, by relating theoretically meaningful process variables to clinical outcomes, can bridge neuroscience and clinical practice. The review emphasizes clinical applications, and the full text was not retrieved here.
