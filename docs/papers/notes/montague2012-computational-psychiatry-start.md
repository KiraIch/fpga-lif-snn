## 一句話重點

把精神疾病視為跨尺度的「計算異常」，並以強化學習與賽局理論作為研究決策功能障礙的起點。

## English summary

This review introduces computational psychiatry as a field that characterizes mental dysfunction in terms of aberrant computations across scales. It highlights early work using reinforcement learning and game theory to study decision-making in health and disease. The full text was not accessed, so specific studies and figures are not confirmed here.
